Delete your LifeDashPro account

You can permanently delete your LifeDashPro account and associated cloud data.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open LifeDashPro and sign in.
  2. Open Settings → Privacy.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. For a password account, enter the current password. For Google sign-in, use a recent sign-in session.
  5. Type DELETE and confirm.

If you cannot access the app

Email monsiai.apps@gmail.com from the email address connected to your LifeDashPro account and request account deletion.

What is deleted

Account deletion removes the LifeDashPro authentication account and associated account-linked data, including profile data, synced notes, finance records, tasks and reminders, journeys, documents and attachment metadata, vehicles, user-created family items, backup snapshots and user-owned cloud attachments.

What may remain

Limited information may be retained only where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or enforcement of applicable agreements. Any such retained information is handled according to the LifeDashPro Privacy Policy.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your cloud account. Use the in-app account deletion option or request deletion using the email above.
