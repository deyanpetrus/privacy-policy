LifeDashPro-specific summary

LifeDashPro (Android package com.lifedashpro.app) is a personal dashboard that may include Notes, Tasks and Reminders, Finance, Documents and attachments, Vehicles, Family items, Travel/Journey planning, Route and Navigation tools, Radio, Maps, Nearby, Weather, Road and Traffic information, Transit, and Safety/World information.

LifeDashPro uses Supabase for account authentication and, where enabled, cloud database, storage, synchronization and server-side functions. Account-linked information may include email address, user identifier, profile settings, language, country/city selections, notes, tasks, reminders, finance records, invoice or document metadata, attachments selected by the user, vehicle records, family items, journeys, reservations, utility records and app preferences.

With permission, LifeDashPro may use approximate or precise location for weather, nearby places, road and safety information, route planning and navigation. During an active navigation session, location may continue to be used while the app is backgrounded where the Android permission and platform rules allow it. Location permissions can be changed in Android settings.

LifeDashPro may schedule Android notifications for Notes, Tasks, manual reminders, Journey items, Finance dates, vehicle/document dates and other user-configured due dates. Exact-alarm capability is used only where available and appropriate for user-requested reminders. Radio playback may continue in the background through Android media services.

Files, photos or documents are accessed only when the user selects or attaches them for an app feature. Depending on the feature, the selected file or its metadata may be stored locally and/or in authenticated user-scoped cloud storage.

LifeDashPro may maintain user-requested or automatic backup snapshots of supported account data to help restore records. Before a restore, LifeDashPro may create an additional safety snapshot. Backup data is protected by account-scoped backend controls and is deleted when the user chooses the in-app Delete my data or Delete account action, subject to the limited retention provisions below.

The current LifeDashPro release does not include a third-party advertising tracker or third-party crash-reporting SDK. If this changes, the policy and applicable Google Play declarations will be updated before or with the relevant release.

11. Account and Data Deletion

LifeDashPro provides two separate controls in Settings → Privacy. Delete my data removes LifeDashPro user-created and synced app data, backup snapshots, and user-owned cloud attachments while keeping the authentication account and basic profile active. Delete account permanently removes the LifeDashPro authentication account together with associated account-linked app data, backup snapshots, and user-owned cloud attachments.

Account deletion can also be requested through the LifeDashPro account deletion page. Deletion of app data while keeping the account can be requested through the LifeDashPro data deletion page. If you cannot use these options, you may contact us using the information below.
