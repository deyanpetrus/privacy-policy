1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications developed and published by the developer Petrushevski ("we", "us", or "the applications"), including radio/audio streaming apps, note-taking and productivity apps with invoicing/document features, personal management apps, and any future application we publish. By downloading and using any of our applications, you agree to the terms described below.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the specific app and the features you use, we may collect:

  • Registration data — email address and password (encrypted), when the app offers account registration or sign-in.
  • User-generated content — notes, invoices, documents, and similar content you enter into the app. This content is stored encrypted and is accessible only to you.
  • Push notification tokens — for apps offering notifications (e.g. reminders, severe weather alerts), a unique device token is generated to deliver notifications.
  • Location data — only for weather-related features, and only if you explicitly grant location access via your device's system permissions.
  • Technical device data — device type, OS version, and app identifiers, used for functionality and troubleshooting purposes.
  • Payment data — should any app or feature become paid in the future, payments are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store payment card data.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide core app functionality (saving notes, streaming radio, syncing invoices, etc.)
  • To authenticate and protect your account, where registration is supported
  • To deliver push notifications you have enabled (reminders, weather alerts)
  • To retrieve data from third-party weather API services, where such a feature is offered
  • To improve and maintain our applications

4. Data storage and security

All user content (notes, invoices, documents) is stored using encryption. Only you can access your content, through your user account. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. Third-party services

Some apps rely on external (third-party) services to provide certain features:

  • Weather API services — for displaying forecasts and severe weather alerts.
  • Google Play Billing — for processing future in-app payments, should paid features become available.
  • Firebase / push notification services — for delivering notifications to your device.

These third parties maintain their own privacy policies governing their processing of data.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. Data is only shared with the services listed in Section 5, to the extent necessary for their operation, or where required by law.

7. Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data and content at any time. Requests to delete your account and data can be sent to our contact email and will be processed within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Children's privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If we learn that we have collected data from a child without appropriate parental consent, we will delete it promptly upon becoming aware.
