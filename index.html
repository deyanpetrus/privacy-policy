Privacy Policy — Balkan Vibes

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Balkan Vibes (the "App"), available for Android through Google Play, developed and maintained by Petrushevski ("we", "us", "our"). By downloading and using the App, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to work without requiring registration or login. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • We do not require you to create a user account.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not store news content or audio streams on our own servers — the App only displays or plays content directly from the sources listed below.

2. Internet Access and Third-Party Sources

To function, the App connects to the internet and communicates directly with:

  • Radio streaming servers of the radio stations featured in the App, in order to play live audio.
  • RSS feeds (news websites) from which article titles and content are retrieved for display in the "News" section.

These third parties (radio stations and news websites) may have their own privacy policies and may log technical information on their servers (such as your IP address when connecting to a stream), which is outside of our control. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies if you have concerns.

3. Permissions Used by the App

  • Internet access (INTERNET) — required for radio streaming and loading news content.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — used to check for an active internet connection.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — used to display playback controls (play/pause) while the radio is playing in the background.
  • Foreground Service — allows radio playback to continue when the App is running in the background.

4. Analytics and Third-Party Services

The App does not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or any third-party tracking services. We do not track user behavior within the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Data Security

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our own servers, the risk of personal data being compromised through our App is minimal.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, along with an updated "Last updated" date.
