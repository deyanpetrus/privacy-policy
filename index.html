Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications, websites, and digital services published by monsiai ("we", "us", or "our"), including apps such as LifeDashPro, BalkanVibes Radio, and other applications that link to this Privacy Policy.

Different apps may use different features. A feature or third-party service described below applies only when it is actually enabled or used in the relevant app.

1. Information We May Process

Account and profile information

If an app offers registration or sign-in, we may process information such as your email address, authentication identifier, profile settings, selected country, language, and preferences. Authentication and backend services may be provided by Supabase or another service provider disclosed in the relevant app.

Location information

With your permission, certain apps may access approximate or precise device location to provide location-based features such as weather, nearby hospitals, pharmacies, shops, fuel stations, ATMs, EV charging points, police stations, nearby hazards, fires, earthquakes, road and border information, traffic-related information, maritime information, local content, or other nearby results.

Location is used only when needed for the requested feature or for a feature you have enabled. Where technically possible, location may be processed on-device or sent only as coordinates necessary to obtain the requested third-party data.

User-created content

Some apps may allow you to create or store content such as notes, tasks, shopping lists, family information, financial entries, bills, reminders, vehicle dates, documents, or other personal records. Depending on the app and feature, this information may be stored locally on your device, in a backend database linked to your account, or both.

Device and technical information

Apps may process limited technical information required for normal operation, such as app version, device platform, language, network status, crash or diagnostic information, notification permission status, and identifiers required by platform or service providers.

2. How We Use Information

  • provide and maintain app functionality;
  • show location-based, safety, travel, weather, nearby, radio, transport, or other requested information;
  • save and synchronize account data and user-created content;
  • send local reminders and notifications that you enable;
  • restore preferences and improve usability;
  • prevent abuse, maintain security, and troubleshoot technical problems;
  • support purchases or subscriptions where offered.

3. Notifications and Reminders

Some apps may schedule local notifications for notes, tasks, bills, vehicle dates, family tasks, reminders, safety information, or other time-based events. You can disable notifications through the app settings or your device settings.

Where push notifications are used, the relevant app may use a platform or third-party notification service. Push notification identifiers are used only to deliver enabled notifications and operate the service.

4. Third-Party Data and Service Providers

Our apps may retrieve information from third-party services. Depending on the app and selected feature, these may include:

  • Supabase – authentication, database, storage, server-side functions, and synchronization;
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass – map and nearby place data;
  • Radio Browser – internet radio station listings and stream URLs;
  • NASA FIRMS – active fire information;
  • AISStream or other maritime providers – vessel position data;
  • Wikipedia / MediaWiki – encyclopedia and location-related content;
  • weather, earthquake, flood, border, road, transport, fuel-price, traffic, energy-grid, public-data, or news providers;
  • Google Play – app distribution, billing, subscriptions, purchases, and platform services.

These providers process requests according to their own terms and privacy policies. A request may include only the information necessary to provide the selected feature, such as coordinates, a country code, search text, station identifier, or route-related parameters.

5. Radio Streaming

Radio applications or radio features may obtain public station listings and resolved stream URLs from Radio Browser or another radio directory. Audio may be streamed directly from the radio station's own server. The station operator or streaming host may receive standard network information such as your IP address as part of delivering the stream.

Some current or future apps may offer optional paid features, premium access, in-app purchases, or subscriptions. Subscription options may include monthly and/or annual billing periods.

For Android apps distributed through Google Play, eligible digital purchases and subscriptions may be processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment card details from Google Play.

Before purchase, the app or store listing will disclose applicable pricing, billing frequency, whether a trial is offered, whether the subscription automatically renews, and how it can be managed or cancelled. Subscription management, cancellation, billing, refunds, and renewal are subject to Google Play's applicable terms and settings unless otherwise stated.

If advertising or analytics services are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated as needed to identify the relevant processing and service providers. Where consent is legally required, appropriate consent controls will be presented.

No internet transmission, cloud service, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore do not promise absolute security or end-to-end encryption unless a specific app or feature explicitly states that it provides it.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers. We may share or transmit limited information to service providers only when necessary to operate a feature, process a purchase, provide requested data, maintain security, comply with law, or protect legal rights.
